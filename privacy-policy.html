隐私政策

更新日期：2026年7月28日

来路相机（LaiLens，以下简称"本应用"）由开发者独立开发并运营。我们深知个人信息对您的重要性，并会尽全力保护您的隐私安全。请您仔细阅读本隐私政策，了解我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

1. 我们收集的信息

本应用遵循"最小化收集"原则，仅收集为提供核心功能所必需的信息：

  • 相机数据：当您使用拍照或录像功能时，本应用通过设备相机实时获取图像和视频数据。这些数据仅用于实时预览和处理（如美颜、滤镜、水印等），处理完成后保存到您的设备本地相册中。数据不会上传到任何服务器。
  • 麦克风数据：当您录制视频时，本应用通过设备麦克风录制音频。音频仅与视频一起保存到您的设备本地，不会上传。
  • 照片库数据：当您使用应用内置相册功能时，本应用会读取您设备本地保存的照片和视频缩略图以供浏览和管理。所有数据均在设备本地处理。
  • 面部数据：当您使用美颜、美妆功能时，本应用使用 Apple Vision 框架在设备本地进行人脸关键点检测。面部数据不会被保存、上传或分享，处理完成后即被丢弃。

2. 信息的使用

我们收集的信息仅用于以下目的：

  • 提供相机拍摄、美颜滤镜、水印添加等核心功能
  • 提供内置相册的浏览、编辑和管理功能
  • 验证和管理您的订阅状态（通过 Apple StoreKit）

3. 信息的存储

  • 本地存储：您拍摄的所有照片和视频均存储在您的设备本地沙盒中，不会上传到云端或任何远程服务器。
  • 订阅信息：您的订阅状态由 Apple App Store 管理，本应用通过 StoreKit 读取订阅验证信息，不存储任何支付凭证或信用卡信息。
  • 偏好设置：您的应用偏好（如滤镜选择、美颜参数、水印设置等）存储在设备本地的 UserDefaults 中，不会同步到云端。

4. 信息的共享

我们不会将您的个人信息出售、出租或交易给任何第三方。在以下情况下，我们可能会共享必要的信息：

  • Apple：订阅购买和恢复通过 Apple App Store 完成，Apple 会处理相关支付和验证信息。
  • 法律要求：如适用法律、法规或法律程序要求时。

5. 第三方服务

本应用使用了以下第三方技术，但这些技术均在设备本地运行，不会将您的数据发送到外部服务器：

  • Apple Vision 框架：用于人脸检测和 OCR 文字识别，完全在设备本地处理。
  • GPUPixel：开源图像处理库，用于美颜滤镜效果，完全在设备本地运行。
  • Apple StoreKit：用于订阅管理，购买流程由 Apple 处理。

6. 您的权利

您对个人信息享有以下权利：

  • 访问权：您可以随时在应用内查看您拍摄的照片和视频。
  • 删除权：您可以在应用内删除照片和视频，删除后数据将永久移除。
  • 权限管理：您可以在系统"设置"中随时撤销相机、麦克风和照片库访问权限。
  • 订阅管理：您可以在系统"设置 → Apple ID → 订阅"中管理或取消订阅。

7. 数据安全

我们采取合理的技术措施保护您的数据安全，包括但不限于：

  • 所有数据均在设备本地处理和存储，不经过网络传输
  • 隐藏相册功能使用 Face ID / Touch ID 加密保护
  • 订阅验证由 Apple StoreKit 安全框架处理

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们不会故意收集儿童的个人信息。如果您认为我们无意中收集了儿童的信息，请联系我们删除。

9. 隐私政策更新

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。更新后的政策将在应用内或本页面发布，并更新顶部的日期。建议您定期查阅本政策了解任何变更。

Privacy Policy

Updated: July 28, 2026

LaiLens (hereinafter referred to as "this App") is independently developed and operated by the developer. We understand the importance of your personal information and will do our best to protect your privacy. Please read this privacy policy carefully to understand how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

This App follows the "minimal collection" principle, only collecting information necessary to provide core features:

  • Camera Data: When you use the photo or video recording feature, this App obtains image and video data in real time through the device camera. This data is only used for real-time preview and processing (such as beauty filters, filters, watermarks, etc.), and saved to your device's local album after processing. Data will not be uploaded to any server.
  • Microphone Data: When you record a video, this App records audio through the device microphone. Audio is only saved together with the video on your device locally and will not be uploaded.
  • Photo Library Data: When you use the built-in album feature, this App reads photo and video thumbnails stored locally on your device for browsing and management. All data is processed locally on the device.
  • Facial Data: When you use beauty or makeup features, this App uses the Apple Vision framework to detect facial keypoints locally on the device. Facial data is not saved, uploaded, or shared, and is immediately discarded after processing.

2. Use of Information

The information we collect is only used for the following purposes:

  • Provide core features such as camera shooting, beauty filters, and watermark addition
  • Provide built-in album browsing, editing, and management features
  • Verify and manage your subscription status (via Apple StoreKit)

3. Information Storage

  • Local Storage: All photos and videos you take are stored in your device's local sandbox and will not be uploaded to the cloud or any remote server.
  • Subscription Information: Your subscription status is managed by Apple App Store. This App reads subscription verification information through StoreKit and does not store any payment credentials or credit card information.
  • Preferences: Your app preferences (such as filter selection, beauty parameters, watermark settings, etc.) are stored in the device's local UserDefaults and will not be synced to the cloud.

4. Information Sharing

We will not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party. In the following circumstances, we may share necessary information:

  • Apple: Subscription purchases and restores are completed through Apple App Store, and Apple will process relevant payment and verification information.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by applicable laws, regulations, or legal proceedings.

5. Third-Party Services

This App uses the following third-party technologies, but these technologies all run locally on the device and will not send your data to external servers:

  • Apple Vision Framework: Used for face detection and OCR text recognition, fully processed locally on the device.
  • GPUPixel: Open-source image processing library, used for beauty filter effects, fully running locally on the device.
  • Apple StoreKit: Used for subscription management, the purchase process is handled by Apple.

6. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right of Access: You can view the photos and videos you have taken in the app at any time.
  • Right to Delete: You can delete photos and videos in the app, and the data will be permanently removed after deletion.
  • Permission Management: You can revoke camera, microphone, and photo library access permissions at any time in system "Settings".
  • Subscription Management: You can manage or cancel subscriptions in system "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions".

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data security, including but not limited to:

  • All data is processed and stored locally on the device, without network transmission
  • The hidden album feature is protected by Face ID / Touch ID encryption
  • Subscription verification is handled by the Apple StoreKit security framework

8. Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us to delete it.

9. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated policy will be published in the app or on this page, with the date at the top updated. We recommend that you review this policy periodically to stay informed of any changes.
